# CASA 1569 — Australian Compliance Working Notes

**Working document only. Final import and retail labelling should be checked against current Australian requirements before commercial shipment/printing.**

## Launch simplification

Prefer pure `Camellia sinensis` products initially.

This reduces complexity compared with multi-ingredient herbal/botanical blends.

## Biosecurity

Australian DAFF guidance indicates pure black/green/white tea made from Camellia sinensis is generally considered minimal biosecurity risk and does not require an import permit, subject to current BICON conditions and possible inspection.

Check BICON for every commercial pathway before shipment:
https://bicon.agriculture.gov.au/

DAFF background:
https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/import/goods/plant-products/importing-plant-products-for-human-consumption

## Food import

Commercial food must also meet applicable Imported Food Inspection Scheme / food-standard requirements.

## Country of origin

Tea/coffee are generally non-priority foods for country-of-origin labelling but still require an appropriate text statement.

ACCC:
https://www.accc.gov.au/business/advertising-and-promotions/country-of-origin-food-labelling

## Australian packing

Packing an imported origin tea in Australia does not automatically justify a `Made in Australia` representation.

Potential factual form, subject to final review:

> Grown and produced in [origin]. Packed in Australia for CASA 1569.

Do not finalise wording until legal/compliance review.

## Claims

Avoid unsupported therapeutic/wellness claims.

Do not describe a tea as:
- detoxifying;
- treating anxiety;
- improving disease;
- therapeutic;

unless the exact claim is legally permitted and substantiated.

FSANZ:
https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/
