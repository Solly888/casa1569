# CASA 1569 — Voice & Copy Guide

## Voice

CASA 1569 speaks quietly.

The writing is:
- concise;
- cultured;
- specific;
- confident;
- warm;
- restrained;
- editorial.

Luxury comes from specificity, not adjectives.

## Preferred language

> Grown on volcanic soil. Shaped by Atlantic air.

> We begin with place.

> A point of departure.

> Tea, traced to origin.

> Every origin has a story. Every story has a route.

> The producer belongs in the story.

## Avoid

- amazing
- exotic
- secret
- hidden
- world's best
- life-changing
- detox
- superfood
- empire nostalgia
- discovery language applied to people/cultures
- unprovable superlatives

## Portuguese language system

Portuguese terms may define key concepts while English explains them:

- CASA — house
- CHÁ — tea
- ROTA — route
- ORIGEM — origin
- COLHEITA — harvest
- CADERNOS — journals

Do not over-Portuguese the site to the point that international customers cannot navigate it.
