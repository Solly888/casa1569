# CASA 1569 — Brand Guide

## Master identity

# CASA 1569
### A House of Origins.

CASA 1569 is a modern Portuguese house curating exceptional products shaped by remarkable places.

Tea is where the house begins.

## Brand essence

**Origin. Ritual. Discovery.**

## Brand proposition

CASA 1569 seeks exceptional tea from remarkable origins and traces each selection back to the landscape, producer, harvest and traditions that shaped it.

## Core idea

> Every origin has a story.  
> Every story has a route.

## Meaning of the name

**CASA** — house, hospitality, gathering, culture and the potential to become larger than tea.

**1569** — a point of departure referencing Gaspar da Cruz's early Portuguese published account of China and chá. It is not the founding date of CASA 1569.

**CHÁ** — the founding product category.

**ROTA** — the provenance architecture through which products are numbered, mapped and documented.

## Positioning

CASA 1569 should feel closer to a contemporary European luxury house, architectural studio or independent editorial publication than to a conventional tea retailer.

### Desired associations

- cultured
- quiet
- precise
- tactile
- architectural
- Portuguese
- global
- origin-led
- contemporary
- timeless

### Avoid

- nautical theme park
- colonial nostalgia
- rustic farmhouse
- colourful tea chain
- wellness startup
- generic DTC template
- faux heritage

## Brand architecture

### CASA 1569
Master brand.

### CHÁ
Founding category.

### ROTA
Numbered origin/product system.

Examples:
- ROTA 001 — São Miguel
- ROTA 002 — Wuyishan
- ROTA 003 — Darjeeling

### CADERNOS DA ROTA
Editorial and cultural publishing layer.

### Future potential
Only when strategically justified:
- Café
- Cacau
- Especiarias
- Casa / objects / ceramics

Tea remains the core launch category.
