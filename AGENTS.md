# CASA 1569 — Agent Operating Instructions

This repository is the source of truth for CASA 1569.

## Before doing work

Read, in this order when relevant:

1. `/README.md`
2. `/brand/brand-guide.md`
3. `/brand/voice.md`
4. `/business/strategy.md`
5. The relevant functional folder.
6. `/decisions/` for decisions that constrain the work.

Do not override an existing documented decision silently.

## Non-negotiable brand principles

- CASA 1569 is a **modern Portuguese house of origins**, not a themed tea shop.
- Tea is the founding category, not necessarily the permanent limit of the house.
- Origin, producer and provenance come before flavour marketing.
- Portuguese heritage must be intelligent and restrained.
- Never romanticise empire or colonialism.
- Never imply CASA 1569 was founded in 1569.
- Never write `EST. 1569` or `SINCE 1569`.
- Never describe people, cultures or producers as being "discovered" by CASA 1569.
- Avoid generic wellness claims and unsupported health claims.
- Avoid fake luxury language.

## Design standard

Default direction:

**Quiet Portuguese Luxury + Maritime Modernism + Old World Contemporary**

Avoid:
- SaaS aesthetics;
- gradients;
- glassmorphism;
- generic startup illustrations;
- bright tea-shop colours;
- pirate / explorer clichés;
- anchors, ropes, ships or compass logos;
- souvenir-style azulejo treatments;
- excessive gold.

## Historical integrity

The historical story is an asset only if it is accurate.

Use careful formulations such as:

> Portugal was present at the beginning of Europe's encounter with chá.

> 1569 represents a point of departure.

Do not state that Portugal invented tea, definitively introduced tea to Europe, or that Catherine of Braganza introduced tea to England.

## Commercial integrity

Supplier names in this repo are prospects unless a document explicitly says `APPROVED`.

A supplier listed in the sourcing pipeline is not a partner until an agreement exists.

Do not display supplier logos publicly without permission.

## Change management

When making a major brand, product, route, supplier or business-model decision, add an ADR-style decision file to `/decisions/`.
