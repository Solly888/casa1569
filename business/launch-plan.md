# CASA 1569 — Pre-Launch Plan

## Phase 1 — Credibility

- Build static public website.
- Register/secure priority domains and handles.
- Conduct trademark clearance for CASA 1569 and CHÁ 1569.
- Establish supplier contact identity.
- Create origin-partner PDF/brief when needed.

## Phase 2 — Sourcing

- Contact A-priority suppliers.
- Request samples and export documentation.
- Blind taste.
- Select one supplier/lot per route.
- Negotiate small pilot orders.
- Obtain permissions for origin/producers/photography.

## Phase 3 — Operations

- appoint import/co-packing workflow;
- finalise packaging supplier;
- label compliance review;
- shipping/3PL decision;
- product costing and margin model.

## Phase 4 — Audience

- launch Journal;
- build waitlist;
- publish historical/origin content;
- document sourcing process without exposing confidential negotiation;
- establish founder/brand social channels.

## Phase 5 — First release

Preferred:
**THE FIRST ROUTE / ROTA 001 — São Miguel**

Followed by the founding collection or discovery set.

Do not artificially manufacture scarcity.

If releases are numbered, the quantities must be truthful.
