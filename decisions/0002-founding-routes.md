# ADR 0002 — Founding Tea Routes

**Status:** Accepted as sourcing hypothesis

## Decision

Research and sample the following founding origins:

1. ROTA 001 — São Miguel, Açores
2. ROTA 002 — Wuyishan / Fujian, China
3. ROTA 003 — Darjeeling, India
4. ROTA 004 — Ceylon Highlands, Sri Lanka
5. ROTA 005 — Wazuka, Kyoto, Japan

Move Assam to:
6. ROTA 006 — Phase 2

## Rationale

The collection should connect directly to the CASA 1569 brand story.

China belongs in the founding collection because the historical point of departure is an early Portuguese encounter with Chinese `chá`.

São Miguel remains the hero because it makes Portuguese tea heritage physical and contemporary.

## Caveat

Route numbers remain provisional until a product is approved for launch.
