# ADR 0001 — CASA 1569 as Master Brand

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Use:

# CASA 1569
## A House of Origins.

as the master brand.

Protect `CHÁ 1569` if commercially/trademark viable, but use CHÁ primarily as the founding category architecture.

Use `ROTA` as the origin/product numbering system.

## Why

CHÁ 1569 is immediately recognisable as tea but constrains future category expansion.

CASA DA ROTA is a strong philosophy and naming system but less immediately international.

CASA 1569 provides:
- luxury-house potential;
- Portuguese identity;
- hospitality meaning;
- expansion beyond tea;
- historical intrigue.

## Consequence

Early communications require a category descriptor so audiences understand the business.
