# CASA 1569 — Supplier RFQ Checklist

Request answers before any production purchase.

## Origin

- Exact estate/garden?
- Region?
- Harvest date/season?
- Cultivar where relevant?
- Production lot?
- Processing method?
- Elevation where meaningful?

## Samples

Request approximately 100–250g of 2–3 current candidate lots.

## Quality documents

Ask for:
- product specification;
- moisture data;
- microbiological testing where available;
- pesticide/residue testing or COA;
- relevant certifications;
- export documentation examples.

## Commercial

Quote:
- sample price;
- 10kg;
- 25kg;
- 50kg;
- 100kg.

Request:
- EXW;
- FOB where applicable;
- payment terms;
- lead times;
- MOQ.

## Australia

Ask:
- prior Australian export experience;
- shipping terms;
- documentation supplied;
- preferred freight pathway.

## Brand / attribution

Ask:
- can tea be supplied entirely unbranded?
- may CASA 1569 identify the estate/producer?
- may approved estate photography be used?
- may farmer/producer stories be published?
- are there geographical-indication rules relevant to naming?

## Continuity

Ask:
- size of current lot;
- expected seasonal availability;
- comparable replacement policy;
- ability to reserve future lots.

## Future

Only after initial validation:
- territory/channel exclusivity;
- annual volume commitments;
- bespoke lots;
- special processing;
- packaging collaboration.
