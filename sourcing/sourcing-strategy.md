# CASA 1569 — Sourcing Strategy

## Procurement principle

Origin before catalogue.

CASA 1569 should preferably work with:
1. producers / estates;
2. grower-exporters;
3. specialist exporters that can name and document the producer/lot.

Avoid anonymous commodity tea where provenance is lost.

## Selection gates

### 1. Provenance
Exact origin, producer, harvest and lot where applicable.

### 2. Cup quality
Samples are evaluated before commercial appeal.

### 3. Export readiness
Supplier can provide appropriate export and food documentation.

### 4. Startup flexibility
Supplier can support sensible first orders.

### 5. Continuity
Supplier can explain replacement lots/harvest cycles.

### 6. Story rights
CASA can accurately tell the origin story and, where agreed, name the producer.

## Recommended process

1. Desk qualification.
2. RFQ + sample request.
3. Document review.
4. Blind tasting.
5. Commercial scoring.
6. Founder approval.
7. Pilot PO.
8. Incoming QA.
9. Packaging.
10. Route release.

## Pilot philosophy

Do not place large orders to obtain better unit economics before product-market evidence exists.

Initial working assumption:
- ROTA 001: approximately 20 kg.
- ROTA 002–005: approximately 10 kg each.

These are planning quantities only and may change after co-packer economics and supplier MOQ responses.
